Make an NFC cart card

Drop your PICO‑8 cart images (.p8.png) below to lay them out as little cartridge cards, then print the A4 sheet and cut along the guides. Everything happens in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Drop cart .p8.png files here
or click to choose. No carts? You'll get blank templates to label by hand.
Print at 100% / Actual size. Then measure the 100 mm ruler on the printed sheet:

Type what the ruler actually measured and the cards rescale so a reprint is exact (some printers shrink the page a few %). Repeat once if it's still off. A CR80 card is 54 × 85 mm, the size of a standard NFC card.
